Chrome extension

Capture a full page. Annotate it. Export it.

FullSite Shot scrolls the active tab, stitches the tiles into one image, and opens a viewer so you can annotate, copy, or download PNG and PDF. The capture exists only while that viewer tab stays open.

No analytics, accounts, advertisements, or developer telemetry. Captures are not saved and are not sent to Root Digital CR or to third parties.
